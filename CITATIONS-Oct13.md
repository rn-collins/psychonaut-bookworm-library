# Citation ledger, Oct 13, 2026

Scope: the 454 released records in `data/library.json` (main). Every citation string in a released record's source fields (sources, citation, source, claimSource, passageSource, requiredReading, reading-list entries) is listed once per distinct string and finding, under the wording it had before Oct 13, 2026. Withheld material was not opened.

How each was checked:

- **Journal articles** were checked against PubMed (NCBI E-utilities) and Crossref by author, title, journal and year. Rows with a DOI link were confirmed online on Oct 13, 2026.
- **Books, laws, court cases, treaties and historical publications** (for example Food of the Gods, the Controlled Substances Act, Gonzales v. O Centro) were confirmed from standard bibliographic knowledge, not by an online catalog lookup. They are marked verified with that note.
- **Organizational, institutional, trade and press sources** (advocacy and market reports, company documents, news sites, Wikipedia) were not checked against primary records and are marked `not checked`. They remain on the pages and should not be read as evidence.
- **Not found**: no real source matching the citation could be located. On the page the citation now carries `[Source not found; under review]`, and where a sentence in the text rests on it, the sentence carries `(Source not found; under review)`.
